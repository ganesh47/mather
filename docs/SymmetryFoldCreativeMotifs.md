# Symmetry Fold creative motifs provenance

Issue: #752 TestFlight feedback asked for Symmetry Fold to feel more creative and fun while teaching symmetry.

This slice adds code-drawn SwiftUI motifs only; no generated raster images, external art, or third-party assets were added. The motif pack is intentionally simple and bilaterally readable under the existing half-mask + 3D fold mechanic:

- heart
- butterfly
- flower
- kite
- leaf
- badge

Design notes:

- The activity remains speech-first and calm; timed challenge stays optional.
- Success copy introduces “line of symmetry” after the fold, not as required pre-reading.
- Wrong-direction and near-match cues are short, gentle, and app-local.
- No new sensors, camera, child media, network calls, or identity data are introduced.
