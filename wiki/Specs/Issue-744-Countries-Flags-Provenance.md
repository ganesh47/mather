# Issue 744 — Countries & Flags starter deck provenance

Starter deck: India, Japan, France, Egypt, Brazil, Australia, Canada, Kenya.

The app bundles project-owned deterministic PNG drawings for the first eight flags. The files were generated directly with vector-style Pillow drawing commands from basic flag geometry, colors, and educational recognition cues. No third-party image files, scraped web assets, emoji art, logos, people, or brand marks were imported.

This avoids ambiguous web-download licensing while keeping the deck offline and legible at Memory card size. The drawings are simplified for child recognition; they are not intended as legal/ceremonial flag specifications.
